Report Bribery
Karnataka Lokayukta — your legal weapon

Demanding or accepting a bribe is a punishable offence under the Karnataka Lokayukta Act, 1984. File a formal complaint — it's free, it's your right, and it works. Download the forms below, fill them, get the affidavit notarised, and submit to the nearest Lokayukta office or mail directly to the Head Office, Bengaluru.
